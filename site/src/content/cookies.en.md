# Cookie policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

This cookie policy explains how **talk2tia.insideapp.it** (the "Site") uses cookies and similar technologies, in
accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Article 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 and the Guidelines
on cookies of the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante) of 10 June 2021.

## Data controller

InsideApp S.r.l., VAT no. 12703330014 ("InsideApp"). Contact: [www.insideapp.it/contact.html](https://www.insideapp.it/contact.html).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local
storage, work in the same way and are covered by this policy.

## Cookies used by the Site

### Strictly necessary (no consent required)

| Name | Provider | Duration | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `talk2tia_consent` | talk2tia.insideapp.it | 6 months | Stores your cookie choices |
| `talk2tia-theme` (local storage) | talk2tia.insideapp.it | Until deleted | Remembers the light or dark theme |

### Analytics (only with your consent)

With your consent the Site uses **Google Analytics 4**, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to produce aggregated
statistics on visits (pages viewed, approximate country, device and browser type). Google signals and advertising
features are disabled and the data is not used for profiling or advertising. The Google Analytics script is **not
loaded at all** until you accept analytics cookies.

| Name | Provider | Duration | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google | 2 years | Distinguishes visitors |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google | 2 years | Keeps the session state |

Data collected by Google may be transferred to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More
information: [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses data from sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Third-party resources

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser contacts Google's servers to download them, but no cookies are set.
Links to GitHub and Ko-fi take you to sites with their own cookie policies.

## Managing your consent

When you first visit, a banner lets you **accept all**, **reject all** or choose by category; rejecting is as easy as
accepting and closing the banner does not imply consent. You can change your choice at any time with
**Cookie preferences** in the footer of every page. If you withdraw consent, the analytics cookies are deleted.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the
[Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Your rights

You can exercise the rights set out in Articles 15–22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection,
portability) by contacting InsideApp, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione
dei dati personali ([www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

InsideApp may update this policy. Significant changes will ask you for consent again.
